Amazon NL/BE/DE listing optimization and keyword research

Listing-ready content for Sunflake's Amazon expansion: Dutch variation listings, a review of the German listings, and a keyword bank I built and scored.

Type
Professional work, Sunflake
Period
Aug – Nov 2026
Tools
Helium 10, Amazon flat files, Seller Central, Amazon Ads
Markets
Amazon NL, DE, BE

Business context

Sunflake sells on Amazon NL, BE and DE. Its products, such as packing cubes, umbrellas, cooling bags and travel accessories, come in colour and size variations.

Problem

Listings need the right keywords and a correct parent/child structure, or they're hard to find and split their reviews. The German listings also needed checking against client feedback.

My role

Optimizing live listings on Amazon NL and BE (titles, bullets, descriptions and backend keywords), keyword research and flat-file preparation, plus fixing listing issues on Amazon.de. I also set up the Amazon BE ad campaigns, following the existing NL campaign structure. Launch strategy, pricing and ad budgets stay with the owner.

Objectives

Search-ready listings in Dutch, corrected listings in German, and a keyword list the team can reuse.

Process

  1. Research keywords with Helium 10 Cerebro and score each on relevance, search volume, trend, title density, competition and ranking competitors.
  2. Collect them in a keyword bank of 200+ terms, with the prioritization formula and criteria written down.
  3. Build Dutch variation listings: a parent SKU with colour variations, titles, item highlights, 5 bullets, a description and generic keywords per child.
  4. Review the Amazon DE content against the category listings report and client feedback: German text next to an English translation, with a status per row for sign-off.
  5. Optimize the live Amazon NL and BE listings, fix listing issues on Amazon.de and set up the Amazon BE campaigns.

Deliverable

Result

Live listings on Amazon NL and BE are optimized with researched keywords, and German changes go through a row-by-row review before they go live.

Key learning

Putting an English translation next to every German line lets the owner approve each line without translating it himself.
