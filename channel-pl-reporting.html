Monthly channel P&L and 3PL cost reporting

A profit-and-loss report for each of 6 sales channels, a sales report per EAN, and an analysis of fulfilment (3PL) costs per channel.

Type
Professional work, Sunflake
Frequency
Monthly
Tools
Omnidock, Mirakl, Bol.com, 3PL portal, Google Sheets
Deadline
First 5 working days

Business context

Each channel charges differently: marketplace commission, fulfilment, storage, platform fees and marketing. A channel that looks good on revenue can lose money after costs.

Problem

Channel costs sat in separate reports, so the owner couldn't compare profit per channel month by month.

My role

Fully mine: collecting, entering, checking and delivering. I wrote the Omnidock procedure as an SOP (version 1.1, July 2026) so it can be repeated exactly.

Objectives

One P&L per channel, in the same format, delivered on time every month.

Process (Omnidock example)

  1. Open the channel's profitability report for the completed month.
  2. Record gross sales, returned sales, cost of goods sold, units sold and units returned.
  3. Record each cost line: fulfilment, storage, marketplace commission, platform commission, marketing.
  4. Check: right month, every required field filled, no formula overwritten, totals compared with the source and any difference explained.
  5. Repeat for Bol.com NL, Bol.com BE, webshop, ANWB and Obelink, then the EAN sales report and the 3PL cost split.

Deliverable

Result

Every channel is measured the same way each month, so the owner can compare channels and see what each one really earns.

Key learning

Writing the SOP while doing the task exposed every step I'd been doing from memory. The quality checklist is the part that prevents errors.
