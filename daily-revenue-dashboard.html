Daily revenue and ad-efficiency dashboard

Each morning I record the previous day's sales and ad spend for every channel. The sheet turns them into the efficiency numbers the owner checks first.

Type
Professional work, Sunflake
Frequency
Every day, 7 days a week
Tools
Google Sheets, seller dashboards, ad platforms
Time
About 8 minutes a day

Business context

Sunflake sells through its webshop, Bol.com, Omnidock, ANWB, Obelink and Amazon NL, BE and DE. It advertises on Facebook, Google, Bol.com and through affiliates.

Problem

Each channel reports in its own dashboard, so total daily revenue, and what advertising returned, was never in one place.

My role

I own the daily input and the first check. A red cell is reported to the owner the same morning.

Objectives

One row per day that shows total revenue, ad spend, and whether advertising is paying back, for the shop and for Bol.com separately.

Process

  1. Copy the formula columns from the previous row into the new date row.
  2. Enter yesterday's gross revenue per channel and the webshop order count.
  3. Enter ad spend per platform.
  4. Check the calculated columns: MER, 7-day MER, 7-day average order value, profit and contribution margin for shop and Bol.com.
  5. Anything red goes to the owner straight away, with a note in the row.

Deliverable

Result

The owner reads one row instead of opening eight dashboards. The rolling 7-day columns smooth out single bad days, so the alarms that remain are real ones.

Key learning

Consistency beats complexity. The same 8 minutes every morning, including weekends, is what makes the trend columns trustworthy.
