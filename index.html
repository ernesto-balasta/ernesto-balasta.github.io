About

From the shop floor to the supply chain

I've worked in retail since 2008: on the sales floor for Adidas in Manila, as a store supervisor leading 18 people for Landmark Group in Doha, and in franchise merchandising. Then I moved into supply chain at ASSA ABLOY: distributor orders, demand forecasting and 5 logistics providers. I reached 98% on-time distributor delivery, cut stockouts by 20% and lowered transportation costs by 15%.

In 2026 I moved into ecommerce, first as an Amazon research VA for a US reseller, then as ecommerce operations specialist for Sunflake. The channels are new; the supply chain habits aren't. Stock needs watching, shipments need chasing, data needs to be right, and the owner needs to know what changed.

My rule: I own the data and the follow-through. Decisions about money and strategy go to the owner, with the facts ready.
