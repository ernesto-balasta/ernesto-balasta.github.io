Weekly Bol.com performance report

A weekly report that sorts every advertised Bol.com product in the Netherlands and Belgium into a status, and gives each flag a next step and an owner.

Type
Professional work, Sunflake
Frequency
Weekly (Tuesdays)
Tools
MarktMentor, Claude (AI-assisted), Google Docs
Since
September 2026

Business context

Sunflake tracks about 20 active products on Bol.com NL and 4 in BE, most with ads running. The catalog mixes strong sellers, seasonal lines such as umbrellas and cooling products, new launches and stock being phased out.

Problem

The data was all in MarktMentor, but nothing showed at a glance which products were losing margin to advertising and which deserved more budget.

My role

I produce the report each week with an AI-assisted workflow connected to MarktMentor, check it, and deliver it to the owner. Ad bids and budgets, price, deactivation and stopping a market are the owner's decisions. The report gives him the facts and a suggested action for each.

Objectives

Catch margin problems early, separate “watch” from “act now”, and show where extra ad budget is safe.

Process

  1. Pull 7-day and 28-day data per product for NL and BE.
  2. Keep products with 10+ orders, or with at least €10 ad spend.
  3. Apply the status rules below, which the owner set.
  4. Treat launches (first 500 units) and phase-out stock separately, so they aren't flagged red by mistake.
  5. Write one action per flagged product, with an owner and a priority.
  6. Add a quick-stats block and compare with last week's report.

Status rules

StatusRuleWhat happens
Early warning (7 days)10+ orders and margin after ads under 20%Watch closely; escalate if it holds
Red (28 days)TACoS over 15% and margin after ads under 15%, or a high return rateEscalated to the owner
YellowBelow a green thresholdMonitored and adjusted
GreenMargin after ads over 25%, TACoS under 10%, 30+ ordersCandidate for more budget

Deliverable

What the report surfaced

These are findings the report brought to the owner's attention, not results I claim as mine.

  • One product stayed red for six weeks in a row and went to the owner as a choice: deactivate the listing, or raise the price.
  • A product with a 25% return rate was flagged as a listing problem, not an advertising one, with ads paused until returns improved.
  • Ads in Belgium were flagged as loss-making for several products while the same products worked in the Netherlands.

Key learning

A report is only useful if every flag ends in an action and a name. Separating launches and phase-outs stopped the report from crying wolf.
