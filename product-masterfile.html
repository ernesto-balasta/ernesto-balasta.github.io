Professional work Project · Product data

One product masterfile for every channel

I merged two older product files into one masterfile that holds every product's data and its status on every channel, keyed on the internal SKU.

Type
Professional work, Sunflake
Date
Built Nov 2026, maintained since
Tools
Google Sheets, channel dashboards and exports
Scope
All SKUs, 9 sales channels

Business context

Products sell on the webshop, Bol.com, Amazon NL/DE/BE, ANWB, Obelink, Omnidock and TikTok Shop. Each channel has its own SKU, price and listing status.

Problem

Product data lived in a master product list and a separate Amazon master file, and the two didn't always agree. Nobody could see in one place which product was live where, at what price.

My role

I designed the structure, merged the data, checked every channel and kept both old files as untouched backups.

Objectives

One trusted source for product data, one view of channel status, and a clear list of what to fix.

Process

  1. Merge both source files into a Products tab, one row per internal SKU, marking every cell where they disagreed for review.
  2. Build a Channels & Prices tab: per SKU, the webshop, Bol.com and Amazon details (channel SKU, ASIN, price, status, stock).
  3. Check the webshop and Bol.com live, and Amazon, ANWB, Obelink, Omnidock and TikTok from exports.
  4. Add an Active Marketplaces Overview, plus a To Be Listed tab that fills in automatically from any SKU marked “Not listed”.
  5. Log every issue in an Action List with a priority and a status.
  6. Carry over the Amazon content, keyword bank and listing template, and add tabs for content translation and the Amazon DE review.

Deliverable

Result

Gaps are now visible at a glance: products not yet listed on a channel, prices that differ between channels, and data conflicts waiting for a decision.

Key learning

Never overwrite the old sources. Keeping them as backups and marking conflicts, not silently picking one value, is what lets the owner trust the merged file.
